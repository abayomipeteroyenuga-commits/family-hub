Kids Corner

Simple ways children can participate in family faith: short reading, drawing, retelling, memory practice, kindness and prayer.

🎨

Draw the Story

Draw one scene from the weekly Bible passage and explain it.

🧩

Put It in Order

Retell three or four events from the story in the right order.

🔎

Find the Reference

Practise finding the Bible book and chapter with adult help.

💛

Kindness Mission

Choose one safe act of kindness at home, church or school.

🧠

Memory Practice

Say the reference and explain what it means in simple words.

🙏

Short Prayer

Children may pray one sentence, pray silently, or simply listen.
